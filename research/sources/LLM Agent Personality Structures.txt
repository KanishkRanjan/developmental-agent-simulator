Taxonomic  Graph  Analysis  (TGA)  is  a  comprehensive  network  psychometrics  framework  
designed
 
to

from
 
the
 
bottom
 
up,

the
 
theoretical

into
 
classical
 
models

.  It  differs  fundamentally  from  traditional  
exploratory
 
factor
 
analysis
 
(EFA)
 
or

component
 
analysis
 
(PCA),
 
which
 
operate
 
top-down
 
by
 
extracting
 
factors
 
that
 
maximize
 
explained
 
variance
 
until
 
no
 
meaningful
 
variance
 
remains
3
.  Traditional  factor  analysis  struggles  profoundly  with  large  item  pools  due  to  two  major  
psychometric
 
artifacts:
 
local
 
dependence
 
and
 
wording
 
effects
2
.  Local  dependence  occurs  
when
 
two
 
items
 
are
 
highly
 
correlated
 
merely
 
because
 
they
 
share
 
synonymous
 
phrasing,
 
not
 
because
 
they
 
measure
 
the
 
same
 
construct.
 
Wording
 
effects
 
occur
 
when
 
respondents
 
exhibit
 
bias
 
toward
 
positively
 
or
 
negatively
 
phrased
 
items,
 
known
 
as
 
acquiescence
 
bias.
 
For
 
example,
 
in
 
the
 
traditional
 
IPIP-NEO,
 
the
 
items
 
"Am
 
afraid
 
to
 
draw
 
attention
 
to
 
myself"
 
(Neuroticism),
 
"Don't
 
like
 
to
 
draw
 
attention
 
to
 
myself"
 
(Extraversion),
 
and
 
"Dislike
 
being
 
the
 
center
 
of
 
attention"
 
(Agreeableness)
 
artificially
 
inflate
 
cross-trait
 
correlations
 
due
 
to
 
shared
 
syntax
 
rather
 
than
 
shared
 
underlying
 
psychological
 
reality
3
.  TGA  mitigates  these  artifacts  through  a  rigorous,  multi-step  algorithm:  1.  Redundancy  Removal :  The  algorithm  first  identifies  and  removes  items  that  violate  local  
independence,
 
ensuring
 
that
 
semantic
 
similarity
 
does
 
not
 
masquerade
 
as
 
psychological
 
correlation.
 2.  Wording  Effect  Mitigation :  TGA  fits  a  random  intercept  factor  model  to  absorb  the  
variance
 
caused
 
by
 
response
 
styles
 
(e.g.,
 
the
 
tendency
 
to
 
agree
 
with
 
negatively
 
worded
 
statements).
 
The
 
residual
 
correlation

of
 
this
 
artifact—is
 
then
 
used
 
for
 
the
 
actual
 
structural
 
analysis
3
.  3.  Exploratory  Graph  Analysis  (EGA) :  Rather  than  extracting  factors,  EGA  constructs  a

where

represented
 
as
 
nodes,

between

 4.  Louvain  Community  Detection :  A  network  algorithm  groups  highly  connected  nodes  
into
 
specific

which
 
form
 
the
 
facets
 
and
 
traits
3
.  5.  Bootstrapping  for  Stability :  The  process  is  repeated  across  500  simulated  samples  to  
calculate
 
"item

.
