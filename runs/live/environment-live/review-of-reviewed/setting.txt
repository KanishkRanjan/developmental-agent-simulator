A family of three shares a small apartment. Priya is 15, has just started at a new school after a move, and is anxious about making friends. The school is a 20-minute walk from home. Her father Arun, 44, works evening shifts as a hospital nurse (roughly 15:00 to 23:00), worries he is not around enough, and keeps in touch with Priya through the family group chat. Her grandmother Meera, 71, lives with them, is warm but a little old-fashioned, and is usually home. In the second week of term Ms. Okafor, the physics teacher who runs the robotics club, invites Priya to join; the club meets every Thursday after school in the school lab. At the end of the first month, Meera sits down with Priya in the kitchen one evening and asks how school is going. Simulate the first two months.
