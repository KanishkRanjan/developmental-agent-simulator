A family of three shares a small apartment. Priya is 15, has just started at a new school after a move, and is anxious about making friends. Her father Arun, 44, works evening shifts as a hospital nurse and worries he is not around enough. Her grandmother Meera, 71, lives with them, is warm but a little old-fashioned, and is usually home. In the second week of term a teacher invites Priya to join the robotics club. Simulate the first two months.
