This ledger identifies what the materials discuss. It does not claim that the proposed mechanisms are implemented or that all research citations are verified.

The first personality layer now has a separate [source and cluster evidence audit](personality-foundation-evidence.md). It documents completed model tests and a newly verified mismatch between the 300-item source of the TGA hierarchy and the 120-item questionnaire used in the existing benchmark.

**Implementation status is now tracked in the [source-to-code fidelity audit](agent_engine/source-to-code-audit.md).** That audit reread the full visible share and all ten available reports, identified departures in the first prototype, and records specific repairs and missing mechanisms. Presence in this design inventory must not be read as implementation completion.

## What I missed in my spoken summaries

I started with birth and family even though the chat's first dependency is defining and expressing a personality in **one** agent. I then described injection methods while leaving out the Big Five facet profile and TGA mapping that supply those methods' targets. Both omissions were errors of summarization. The table above preserves the full sequence so the next step need not depend on recalling a compressed oral explanation.

## Material visibility limits

The corrected shared chat exposes its own discussion and historical tool-result summaries, but not every historical tool output or attachment. The two additional linked lifespan and mood/memory reports have now been read in full as extracted text during the source-to-code audit; this does not mean every underlying scientific citation has been independently verified. The broad mathematical research prompt is visible; its result is not shown. The separately supplied mental-health PDF is included in the reading audit but is not integrated into the accepted architecture by the visible chat. None of these source-coverage facts should be mistaken for evidence that their scientific claims hold.
